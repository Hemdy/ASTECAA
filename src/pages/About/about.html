Our Legacy

One vision, one campus, one shared journey.

In the classrooms of Owerrinta, our journey began with a shared commitment to academic excellence, integrity, and spiritual growth. What started as individual student paths quickly forged into lifelong bonds, built on the foundations of hard work, technical skill, and the unforgettable moments that defined our school days.

As we stepped past the school gates into the wider world, that original camaraderie evolved into a powerful global network. Today, the ASTEC Alumni Association spans generations of graduates who continue to support one another, mentor the next generation, and give back to our beloved alma mater through impactful development projects.

This portal is the home for all of it: the memories, the milestones, the reunions, and the collective future we are building together.
