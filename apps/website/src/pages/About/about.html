Our Foundations

Forged in Owerrinta, Connected Across the Globe

In the classrooms, technical workshops, and chapel halls of Owerrinta, our journey began with a shared commitment to academic excellence, craftsmanship, and Christian integrity.

What started as individual students walking the campus paths quickly forged into unbreakable lifelong bonds. As graduates stepped through the gates into the wider world, that camaraderie evolved into a dynamic global alumni network supporting each other through every chapter of life.

Today, ASTECAA serves as the collective home for all of it: the preserved memories, the milestones, the reunions, and the ongoing investments that keep our Alma Mater thriving for the generations to follow.

Strategic Purpose

Our Mission

To unite ASTECAA alumni across graduating sets, foster meaningful fellowship, provide mentorship and professional opportunities, and mobilize resources to advance the academic and physical development of ASTEC.

  • Uniting alumni across generations and geographic chapters.
  • Mentoring young graduates and supporting career progression.
  • Championing infrastructure and academic enhancements at ASTEC.
Enduring Aspiration

Our Vision

To be an exemplary, globally respected alumni association that preserves the heritage of our Alma Mater, fosters visionary leaders, and creates transformative impact for students and communities worldwide.

  • Establishing a sustainable endowment for deserving ASTEC scholars.
  • Fostering professional excellence in tech, sciences, arts, and enterprise.
  • A permanent living archive preserving 30+ years of school heritage.
